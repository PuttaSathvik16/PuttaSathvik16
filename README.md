### 🧠 About Me

I design and ship production-grade AI systems. Currently an **AI/ML Engineer at Virtusa**, staffed on the **UnitedHealth Group** account, where I work on LLM-powered applications, multi-agent workflows, and retrieval pipelines for regulated enterprise environments.

Before moving into AI engineering, I spent **3+ years as a Data Analyst / Data Engineer** across financial services and IT consulting (Kestra Financial, Persistent Systems), building large-scale ETL pipelines, fraud detection models, and analytics platforms on Spark, Snowflake, and Azure. That foundation in data is what makes my AI systems reliable in practice, not just in demos.

- 🎓 M.S. in Computer Science & Information Technology, Sacred Heart University (GPA 3.92)
- 🏢 AI/ML Engineer @ Virtusa · UnitedHealth Group account · Hartford, CT

- 🔭 Currently deep in **LangGraph, MCP (Model Context Protocol), agent memory architectures, and agentic RAG**
